Fountain Screenplay Format

Extension: FountainExtension
Syntax: ```fountain
Config Option: fountain (enabled by default)

Overview

Complete implementation of the Fountain screenplay format specification for writing screenplays in plain text.

Format Specification

Scene Headings

Automatic Detection: Lines starting with: INT, EXT, EST, INT./EXT, INT/EXT, I/E (case insensitive)

INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY
EXT. PARK - NIGHT
INT./EXT. CAR - CONTINUOUS

Forced Scene Heading: Prefix with a period (.)

SNIPER SCOPE POV
FLASHBACK

Scene Numbers: Append with number (automatically stripped)

INT. HOUSE - DAY
INT. HOUSE - DAY
INT. HOUSE - FLASHBACK (1944)

Characters

Automatic Detection: - Line in ALL CAPS - Preceded by blank line - NOT followed by blank line

JOHN
Hello there.
SARAH
(smiling)
You're late.

Character Extensions: Parenthetical notations after character name

MOM (O.S.)
Luke! Come down for supper!
HANS (on the radio)
What was it you said?

Forced Character: Prefix with @ to preserve mixed case

McCLANE
Yippie ki-yay!

Dialogue

Text following a Character or Parenthetical element.

SANBORN
A good 'ole boy. You know, loves the Army,
blood runs green. Country boy. Seems solid.

Parentheticals

Wrapped in parentheses, between Character and Dialogue.

STEEL
(starting the engine)
So much for retirement!

Dual Dialogue

Add ^ after second character name for simultaneous dialogue.

BRICK
Screw retirement.
STEEL
Screw retirement.

Action

Any paragraph that doesn't meet other element criteria.

They drink long and well from the beers.
And then there's a long beat.
Longer than is funny.

Forced Action: Prefix with ! when action is in uppercase

!BRICK THROWS THE BOOK

Transitions

Automatic Detection: - ALL CAPS - Ends with TO: - Preceded and followed by blank line

CUT TO:
FADE TO:
DISSOLVE TO:

Forced Transition: Prefix with >

Burn to White.

Lyrics

Lines starting with ~

Willy Wonka! Willy Wonka!
The amazing chocolatier!

Centered Text

Wrapped in > and <

THE END
TITLE CARD

Page Breaks

Three or more equals signs

* * *

Title Page

Key-value pairs at document start

BRICK & STEEL
FULL RETIRED
Written by
Stu Maschwitz
1/20/2012
1588 Mission Dr.
Solvang, CA 93463

Supported Keys: - Title, Subtitle, Credit, Author(s) - Source, Draft date, Contact - Copyright, Notes

Notes

Enclosed in double brackets (stripped from output)

INT. TRAILER HOME - DAY
This is the home of THE BOY BAND, AKA DAN and JACK.

Emphasis

Following Markdown conventions:

italic
bold
bold italic
underline

Combined emphasis:

From what seems like only INCHES AWAY. Steel's face FILLS the Leupold Mark 4 scope.

Escaping:

\9765\

Complete Example

My Screenplay
John Doe
1/22/2026
INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY
The morning rush is over. A few patrons sit scattered about.
JOHN enters, looking around nervously.
SARAH sits at a corner table, coffee in hand. She waves.
SARAH
(smiling)
You're late.
JOHN
Traffic was terrible.
He sits down across from her.
SARAH
I ordered for you.
She slides a coffee across the table.
JOHN
Thanks. Listen, about last night...
SARAH
(interrupting)
We don't have to talk about it.
JOHN
But I want to.
A long beat. They look at each other.
TITLE CARD: THREE YEARS LATER
FADE TO:
EXT. PARK - DAY
* * *

CSS Classes

  • .fountain-screenplay - Main container
  • .title-page - Title page container
  • .title-* - Title page elements (title, author, etc.)
  • .scene-heading - Scene headings
  • .character - Character names
  • .character.dual - Dual dialogue character
  • .dialogue - Dialogue text
  • .parenthetical - Parentheticals
  • .action - Action/description
  • .transition - Transitions
  • .centered - Centered text
  • .lyrics - Lyrics
  • .page-break - Page breaks
  • .blank-line - Blank lines

Styling

Fountain output uses Courier New font and standard screenplay formatting: - Scene headings: Bold, uppercase - Character names: Bold, uppercase, indented 2in - Dialogue: Indented 1.5in left, 1.5in right - Parentheticals: Indented 1.8in left - Transitions: Bold, uppercase, right-aligned - Centered text: Center-aligned - Lyrics: Italic, indented 2in
